About ARC

AIUB Robotics Crew

Competent Engineers from diverse backgrounds coming together to build a future for Robotics in Bangladesh.

ARC is a student club from American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB). We have over a decade of history in the Robotics arena at both the national and international landscape with several notable achievements.

Our Mission

Build world‑class robots, inspire the next generation, and put Bangladesh on the global robotics map.

Our Vision

Become a globally recognised hub for robotics research and innovation, fostering excellence and collaboration.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

2018 Founded at AIUB
2020 First URC qualification
2022 South Asian Champions
2025 International Finalist & Best Autonomous System
