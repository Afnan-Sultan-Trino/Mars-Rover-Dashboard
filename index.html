About ARC

AIUB Robotic Crew

Engineering the Future of Planetary Exploration — From Bangladesh to the World

The AIUB Robotic Crew (ARC) is the premier robotics team of the American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB). We compete in the University Rover Challenge (URC) — the world's premier Mars rover competition — and have earned international recognition, including URC 2025 Finalist and the Best Autonomous System Award.

Our Mission

Build world‑class robots, inspire the next generation, and put Bangladesh on the global robotics map.

Our Vision

Become a globally recognised hub for robotics research and innovation, fostering excellence and collaboration.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

2018 Founded at AIUB
2020 First URC qualification
2022 South Asian Champions
2025 International Finalist & Best Autonomous System

Innovation Lab

Research & Development

Pushing the boundaries of robotics through cutting-edge research and collaboration.

🤖

Autonomous Navigation

Developing robust SLAM algorithms, obstacle detection, and path planning for unstructured terrain. Our systems enable rovers to navigate autonomously in Mars-like environments.

Active
🧬

AI-Driven Science

Using machine learning to analyze geological samples, detect biosignatures, and classify terrain features — accelerating scientific discovery on planetary missions.

Active
🛠️

Robotic Manipulation

Researching dexterous manipulation, force control, and adaptive gripping for robotic arms to perform complex tasks like sample collection and instrument deployment.

Ongoing
📶

Communication Systems

Exploring reliable, low-latency communication protocols for remote rover operations, including mesh networking and delay-tolerant networking.
