DRONE WORKSHOP

About the Workshop

Dive into the world of aerial robotics! Learn the fundamentals of drone mechanics, flight controllers, aerodynamics, and programming. This hands-on workshop will take you from assembling your own quadcopter to mastering manual flight and autonomous navigation.

Whether you are a beginner curious about drones or an enthusiast looking to refine your skills, our experienced mentors will guide you every step of the way. You'll learn to troubleshoot, repair, and eventually program your drone for automated flight paths.
