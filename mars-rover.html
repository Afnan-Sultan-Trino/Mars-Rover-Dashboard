Brief Overview

ARC Rover Team is the flagship rover division of AIUB Robotic Crew — a student-run engineering group building autonomous, science-capable rovers to compete internationally and push Bangladeshi robotics forward.

🧭

Autonomous Navigation

GPS-denied terrain traversal using computer vision, LiDAR mapping, and obstacle-aware path planning.

🔬

Science Mission

On-site soil analysis and life-detection instrumentation built and calibrated entirely by our science team.

📦

Delivery Mission

Precision payload transport and equipment servicing tasks that simulate real astronaut-support scenarios.

🦾

Arm Manipulation

A dexterous robotic arm engineered for fine sample collection and tool operation under mission constraints.

History & Achievements

From a small group of curious students in 2014 to Bangladesh's first internationally-competing rover team — here's the trail we've left behind.

2014

ARC founded by a small group of AIUB engineering students.

2016

First promotional rover demo and prototype chassis built in-house.

2019

Selected among the world's top teams for the International Rover Challenge — first Bangladeshi entry.

2022

South Asian Champions — regional title secured ahead of the finals.

2025

Best Autonomous System Award — first Bangladeshi team to win a URC category.
