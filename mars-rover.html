Brief Overview

ARC Rover Team is the flagship rover division of AIUB Robotic Crew — a student-run engineering group building science-capable rovers to compete internationally and push Bangladeshi robotics forward. AIUB Robotic Crew is the only team from Bangladesh to compete at the Mars Desert Research Station (MDRS) for the fourth time in a row.

The University Rover Challenge (URC), organized by the Mars Society, challenges university students from all over the world to design and build the next generation of Mars rovers that will one day work alongside astronauts exploring the Red Planet. It is held every summer at MDRS, outside Hanksville, Utah, USA — a site chosen for its Mars-like barren desert terrain and soil chemistry similar to that of Mars. Rovers perform teleoperated or autonomous tasks that would assist astronauts in the field, with operators working from control stations that have no direct view of the rover.

History & Achievements

From the very first AIUB Robotic Crew team in 2016 to the 2019 finals — four years of growth in knowledge and experience, with each generation inspiring the next to build a rover capable of traversing an extraterrestrial planet and conducting scientific studies on its surface.

2016

The very first AIUB Robotic Crew team — a milestone for the university. They stood 8th among participants from all over the world in the Phobos final, and were invited to display their rover at the US Embassy.

2017

Returned to MDRS, Hanksville, Utah for the 2nd time. Scored the 2nd-highest mark in the Critical Design Review (CDR) and finished 32nd overall in the world.

2018

A team of 14 Electrical students and 5 Computer Science students placed 26th among 93 teams worldwide, and set a mark by hosting outreach programs for school students interested in robotics.

2019

12 students from EEE and Computer Science qualified for the final round as the top scorer among all Bangladeshi teams — one of 36 teams from 10 countries selected after the most competitive System Acceptance Review (SAR) in URC history.
