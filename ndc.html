NDC WORKSHOP

About the Workshop

Notre Dame College (NDC) workshop is designed to push your creative and engineering limits. You'll be tasked with designing and building a robot to solve a specific, complex problem within a limited timeframe.

This workshop emphasizes rapid prototyping, teamwork, and innovative problem-solving. You'll learn to think on your feet, utilize limited resources effectively, and present your engineering solutions to a panel of judges.
