ROBO-SOCCER

About the Workshop

Experience the thrill of competitive robotics! Robo-Soccer combines mechanical design, electronics, and strategic gameplay. Build a robot capable of dribbling, passing, and scoring goals against opponents in a fast-paced arena.

This workshop covers motor control, wireless communication, and chassis design. You'll learn how to optimize your robot for speed, torque, and agility, preparing you for national and international Robo-Soccer competitions.
