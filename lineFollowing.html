LINE FOLLOWING

About the Workshop

Master the art of autonomous navigation! In this workshop, you'll learn how to build a robot that can follow a black line on a white surface (or vice versa) using infrared sensors and PID control algorithms.

This is the perfect starting point for anyone interested in autonomous vehicles. You'll gain hands-on experience with sensor calibration, motor drivers, and writing efficient code to keep your robot on track at high speeds.
